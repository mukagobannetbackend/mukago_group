# Mukago Group Website Plan

## Subsidiaries
1. **Mukago Capital**: Investment and financial services.
2. **Mukago Properties**: Real estate and property management.
3. **Mukago Wallstreet Schools**: 
   - Primary School
   - Secondary School
   - Institution (Higher Learning)
4. **Mutoto Manufacturers**: Manufacturing and production.
5. **Mukago Technologies**: Tech solutions and innovation.

## Design Specs
- **Theme**: Dark background with bright/vibrant accents (Gold/Cyan/White).
- **Login Page**: Advanced dark theme, sleek UI.
- **Images**: High-quality, "advanced" type offices and professional settings.
- **Backend**: Flask (Python) for routing, user authentication, and data management.

## Page Structure
- Home (Overview of all subsidiaries)
- About Us
- Subsidiaries (Dropdown/Grid)
  - Capital
  - Properties
  - Schools (Primary, Secondary, Institution)
  - Manufacturing
  - Technologies
- Media/News
- Contact Us
- Login/Register (Dark theme)
- Dashboard (For logged-in users)
